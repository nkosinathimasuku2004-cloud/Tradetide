How TradeTide Works

Simple steps to exchange skills and services effectively

1

Create Your Profile & List Your Skills

Start by setting up your free TradeTide profile. Showcase your talents, experience, and the services you're offering. The more detailed your profile, the easier it is for others to find you!

  • Highlight your expertise (e.g., "Web Design", "Photography", "Tutoring").
  • Add a compelling description of what you can do.
  • Upload a professional profile picture.
2

Browse Skills & Services Near You

Explore a wide range of skills and services offered by our vibrant community. Use our powerful search and filter options to find exactly what you need, whether it's local or remote.

  • Search by category, keyword, or location.
  • View profiles and reviews of other members.
  • Save interesting services to your watchlist.
3

Propose a Barter or Cash Transaction

Found what you're looking for? Reach out to the provider and propose a trade! You can offer your own skills in exchange, or discuss a fair cash payment for their services.

  • Initiate a secure chat with the other party.
  • Clearly define the terms of your proposed exchange.
  • Negotiate and finalize the agreement.
4

Collaborate, Complete, and Leave Feedback

Once an agreement is reached, it's time to collaborate! Work together to complete the service. After completion, both parties can leave reviews to build trust within the community.

  • Communicate effectively through the platform.
  • Mark the barter as complete when done.
  • Share your experience and rate the other user.
